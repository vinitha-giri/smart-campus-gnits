# GNITS Smart Campus - Enterprise UI Update

## Preserved functionality
- Role-based login (Admin, Faculty, Student)
- Timetable Excel upload/validation and multi-sheet support
- Live occupancy
- Room management
- Room booking for Admin/Faculty
- My bookings
- Reports and Excel export

## UI/logic improvements
- Responsive desktop/mobile navigation
- VS Code-style hover-expand sidebar with interactive draggable scrollbar
- Functional top search and profile/sign-out menu
- Dashboard KPI cards: total, available now, occupied now, maintenance
- Live room status refreshed automatically
- Room cards/table show availability until the next scheduled/booking event
- Occupancy time slots are loaded from the actual timetable; arbitrary timetable intervals such as 09:20-10:20 are supported
- Reports filters support academic year, semester and section
- Reports show Scheduled Today, Active Now, Bookings Today and Available Now
- Weekly day cards are compact and clickable; selecting a day loads its actual timetable and bookings
- Utilization is retained as a secondary management metric, not as the room availability decision
